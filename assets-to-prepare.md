# Assets to prepare before the next phase

Please gather these and send them to your designer (WhatsApp, email or a Google Drive link). Send them as **original files** where you can, not screenshots.

- [ ] Logo
- [ ] Original/high-resolution logo
- [ ] Other logo versions
- [ ] Product photos
- [ ] Product videos
- [ ] Owner/founder photo
- [ ] Workspace photos
- [ ] Customer photos (with permission)
- [ ] Social media links
- [ ] Product price list
- [ ] Product catalogue
- [ ] Business documents (where appropriate)
- [ ] Existing promotional material
- [ ] Testimonials
- [ ] Brand inspiration (links, screenshots, Pinterest board)
- [ ] Other relevant files

## Tips

- **Photos:** send the original photo from your phone or camera. When photos are sent through WhatsApp they get squashed. Use “Send as document” if you can, or share a Google Drive folder.
- **Logo:** the best files end in `.svg`, `.ai`, `.eps`, `.pdf` or a large `.png`. Ask whoever designed it.
- **Price list:** a photo of your handwritten list is fine, or a spreadsheet.
- **Customer photos & testimonials:** only include them if the customer agreed, and remove phone numbers.
- **Do not send** ID documents, bank statements or passwords.
