# How to complete your questionnaire

Hello Mimah 💜

This questionnaire helps me understand you, your craft and your customers *before* I design anything, so the website feels like Mimah's Atelier and not like a template. There are no wrong answers.

## Before you start
- Set aside **45–60 minutes**, but you do **not** have to finish in one sitting.
- Use a **phone or a computer**; both work. A cup of tea helps ☕
- Have your phone nearby: you may want to look up your Instagram name, WhatsApp number, or a few prices.

## Opening it
1. Open the file called **`index.html`** (double-click it, or open the link I send you). It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
2. Tap **“Let's begin”**.

## Answering
- **Required** questions (purple label) matter most. There are only 34 of them. **Optional** questions can be skipped.
- Under most questions you'll see small buttons:
  - **Not sure yet**: you haven't decided or don't know. That's a valid answer.
  - **Not applicable**: it doesn't apply to your business.
  - **Please help me write this**: you'd like me to write that wording for you.
- Write the way you talk. Short answers are fine. Long stories are even better.
- Lists with boxes: tick everything that applies. Lists with round buttons: choose one.
- Under the **product list** (Section 3), add one card per product. If you already have a price list, skip it and send me the list instead.
- **File upload boxes** only remember file names. You'll send the actual photos and files to me separately (see “Sending your files”).

## Saving and coming back
- Everything is **saved automatically on your device** as you go. You'll see “✓ Saved on this device” at the top.
- To continue later, open the same file or link **on the same phone/computer and browser** and tap **“Continue where I left off”**.
- Want to switch devices? Tap **“Download my answers (backup)”** on the review page, then on the new device tap **“Load a saved file”** on the welcome page.
- **Privacy:** your answers stay on your device. They are only sent when *you* press Submit (or send me the file yourself). Please **don't type passwords, ID numbers or bank/EcoCash numbers** anywhere.

## Moving around
- Use the list of sections on the left (on a phone, tap the ☰ button at the top).
- The thread bar at the top shows how far you've come.
- A section's circle shows ✓ when done, ! when a required question still needs you.
- If you skip a required question, you'll get a friendly reminder, and you can still continue and come back later.

## Finishing
1. Go to **Review & submit**. Look through your answers and tap **Edit** to change any of them.
2. Tap **Submit my questionnaire**. You'll need all required questions answered (or marked “Not sure yet”).
3. On the thank-you page tap **Download my answers (.json)**. You can also **print or save as PDF**.
4. **Send me the downloaded file** by WhatsApp or email, together with the files from your checklist.

## Sending your files
Please see **`assets-to-prepare.md`** (or Section 23). Send original photos using “Send as document” on WhatsApp, or share a Google Drive folder, because normal WhatsApp sharing makes photos blurry.

## If you'd rather use paper
Open **`01-questionnaire-printable.html`** in your browser and print it, fill it in by hand, then send me clear photos of the pages.

## Stuck?
Skip it, tap “Not sure yet”, and message me. We can go through any section together by phone or video call.
